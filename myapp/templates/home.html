About Us

Waste collection that is easy to request, track and pay for

TakaSmart Kenya connects residents, waste collectors and waste management officers on one platform. Residents book a pickup, officers assign the right collector, and everyone sees the status in real time.

Payments go through M-Pesa, so there is no cash handling and every transaction has a record.
